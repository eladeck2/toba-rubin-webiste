I’m Tova Rubin. For twenty years I worked as a clinical psychologist in private practice in the Washington, DC area.

In that work we explored the mind, and sometimes the body, through what people felt and sensed. What we rarely touched was self-care: how we eat, move, rest and look after ourselves day to day.

It was time for more integration. So I trained as a plant-powered chef, and today I bring both parts of my life to one table in North Bethesda. I offer the 360 Assessment, give talks and workshops, lead retreats and host private cooking parties.

## The 360 view

I look at six areas of life together, because they work together: food, movement, mental wellness, sleep, purpose and relationships. Food is where I start. A shared meal opens the door to everything else, and practices like meditation are woven into the work.

## Training and experience

- PhD, Vanderbilt University, with distinction
- Residency and postdoctoral fellowship in San Antonio, Texas
- Twenty years as a licensed clinical psychologist in private practice
- Former adjunct faculty, George Washington University
- Former hospice volunteer
- Certified plant-based chef

## The book

*Wisdom from the Couch* (2020) gathers short lessons from my years in practice. It covers creating clear, healthy boundaries, stepping away from relationships that harm you, and managing the thoughts that run you.

## How I work now

My psychology training shapes everything I do. I no longer offer psychotherapy. What I offer now is simpler: good food, honest conversation, and practical ways to live a little better.
