I’m Tova Rubin. For twenty years I worked as a clinical psychologist in private practice in the Washington, DC area.

I listened to people carry stress, worry, grief and hope. Again and again I saw how much the basics shape how we feel: what we eat, how we move, how we sleep, and who we share our lives with.

So I trained as a plant-based chef. Today I bring both parts of my life to one table. I teach cooking classes, lead retreats, give talks and write.

## The 360 view

I look at six areas of life together, because they work together: food, movement, mental wellness, sleep, purpose and relationships. Food is where I start. A shared meal opens the door to everything else.

## Training and experience

- PhD, Vanderbilt University, with distinction
- Residency and postdoctoral fellowship in San Antonio, Texas
- Twenty years as a licensed clinical psychologist in private practice
- Adjunct faculty, George Washington University
- Hospice volunteer
- Certified plant-based chef

## The book

*Wisdom from the Couch* (2020) gathers short lessons from my years in practice. It covers creating clear, healthy boundaries, stepping away from relationships that harm you, and managing the thoughts that run you.

## What I offer, and what I don’t

I’m a psychologist by training, and that training shapes everything I do. I no longer offer psychotherapy, diagnosis or medical advice. What I offer now is simpler: good food, honest conversation, and practical ways to live a little better.
